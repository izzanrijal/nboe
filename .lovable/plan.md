## Struktur (mengikuti 02_ep_aritmia.md persis)

1. **SCOPE & BLUEPRINT STATION** — posisi di ujian board, kompetensi (ESC 2023 ACS, ESC 2021 AHF, AHA 2020 ACLS, SCAI 2019 cardiogenic shock stages, ELSO ECMO), perangkat (monitor, defibrilator, ventilator, IABP, Impella, VA-ECMO, Swan-Ganz, POCUS), cara skoring 0-2.

2. **HIGH-YIELD KNOWLEDGE**
   - 2.1 STEMI — door-to-balloon ≤90 menit, fibrinolitik bila PCI >120 menit, DAPT, kontraindikasi fibrinolitik.
   - 2.2 NSTEMI/UA — GRACE, TIMI, strategi invasif (immediate <2 j, early <24 j, delayed <72 j).
   - 2.3 Cardiogenic shock — SCAI A–E, hemodinamik (CI <2.2, PCWP >15, SBP <90), inotrop/vasopresor (norepi first-line, dobutamin, milrinone), MCS (IABP, Impella CP/5.5, VA-ECMO), DanGer Shock trial 2024.
   - 2.4 Acute decompensated HF — wet/cold matrix Forrester-Stevenson, diuretik IV, ultrafiltrasi, vasodilator (NTG, nesiritide).
   - 2.5 Henti jantung — ACLS algoritma (VF/pVT, PEA/asistol), epinefrin 1 mg q3-5min, amiodaron 300 mg, post-arrest TTM 32-36°C 24 jam, koroner urgent bila STEMI.
   - 2.6 Aritmia emergensi — narrow QRS tachy (adenosin, valsalva), wide QRS (amiodaron/procainamide, kardioversi), torsades (Mg 2 g, isoproterenol, overdrive pacing), bradi (atropin, transcutaneous pacing).
   - 2.7 Hipertensi emergensi — nicardipine, clevidipine, esmolol, labetalol, target MAP turun 25% jam pertama; aortic dissection (HR <60, SBP <120).
   - 2.8 Tamponade — Beck triad, pulsus paradoxus >10 mmHg, perikardiosentesis subxiphoid panduan echo.
   - 2.9 Massive PE — sPESI, RV strain echo, troponin/BNP, fibrinolitik (alteplase 100 mg/2 j atau 50 mg low-dose), katater-directed (EKOS), surgical embolectomy.
   - 2.10 Endocarditis akut — Duke 2023, indikasi bedah dini (HF, abses, vegetasi >10 mm + emboli).
   - 2.11 ECMO/MCS — VA vs VV, indikasi, kontraindikasi, weaning, komplikasi (Harlequin, LV venting).
   - 2.12 Sepsis dengan disfungsi jantung & takotsubo akut.

3. **SKENARIO OSCE (5)** — tiap skenario: profil pasien, vital, EKG/lab/echo POCUS, 4-6 pertanyaan penguji, dialog kandidat, rubrik 18-22 item × skala 0-2.
   - **Skenario 1 — STEMI Anterior + Killip III**: laki 58 thn, nyeri dada 2 jam, EKG STE V1-V6, ronki basal, SpO2 88%. Manajemen DAPT load, antikoagulan, primary PCI, NIV, diuretik.
   - **Skenario 2 — Cardiogenic Shock SCAI D pasca-MI**: wanita 65, post-PCI LAD 24 jam, SBP 78/50, laktat 5.2, CI 1.6, PCWP 24. Eskalasi norepi → dobutamin → Impella vs VA-ECMO, diskusi DanGer Shock.
   - **Skenario 3 — Cardiac Arrest VF + ROSC**: kandidat memimpin code blue (algoritma ACLS), ROSC tercapai → TTM, koroner emergensi, prognostik neurologi 72 jam.
   - **Skenario 4 — Massive PE dengan hemodinamik tidak stabil**: laki 45, post-op ortopedi, SBP 82, RV/LV >1, troponin (+). Pilihan reperfusi (sistemik fibrinolitik vs CDT vs surgical), kontraindikasi.
   - **Skenario 5 — Aortic Dissection Tipe A**: laki 60, nyeri dada robek menjalar punggung, TD 190/110, mediastinum lebar. Stabilisasi (esmolol → nicardipine), CT angio, konsultasi bedah segera, manajemen nyeri, diferensial Stanford A vs B.

4. **APPENDIX A–L**: Algoritma ACLS lengkap, tabel dosis vasopresor/inotrop (μg/kg/min), kriteria SCAI A-E dengan biomarker, tabel MCS comparison (IABP vs Impella CP vs Impella 5.5 vs VA-ECMO), kriteria fibrinolitik PE (PESI, sPESI), Killip & Forrester class, TIMI/GRACE score, DAPT durations, target TTM, rumus Wells/Geneva PE, daftar landmark trials (DanGer Shock 2024, ECLS-SHOCK 2023, IABP-SHOCK II, PEITHO, TTM2, COACT, EARLY-NSTEMI, ISAR-REACT 5), red flags, ringkasan **A-B-C-D-E** untuk 5 menit terakhir.

5. **MCQ/SAQ Bank** 20 soal dengan kunci jawaban + penjelasan (ACS, shock, ACLS, PE, dissection, ECMO).

6. **H-1 Cheat Sheet** — angka kunci (D2B ≤90 min, TTM 32-36°C, norepi 0.05-0.5 μg/kg/min, alteplase 100 mg/2 j, SCAI thresholds, PCWP >15 cardiogenic, MAP target post-arrest ≥65).
